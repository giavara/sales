Three layers of demand.

People already searching for you.

The easy wins: buyers typing exactly what you sell. We own these keywords first and convert them.

fuel test kit diesel tester water absorber jet fuel test fuel contamination test

People buying around you.

The real upside: buyers who never search your product but purchase adjacent gear. We place you right next to it — the natural companion buy.

diesel pump fuel transfer pump storage tank cistern / IBC jerry can fuel storage

Cutting the wasted spend.

Some keywords surface domestic water filters and unrelated products. We exclude them aggressively so budget and ACOS stay under control.

water filter home brita jug drinking water shower filter aquarium

Ad budget (your side): €1.000 / month per channel to start — enough to test properly. Once we read the data it settles lower: it always tracks your sales and stays within the ROAS target we agree.

The metrics we'll use — ROAS = revenue ÷ ad spend · ACOS = ad spend ÷ ad sales · TACOS = ad spend ÷ total sales.
